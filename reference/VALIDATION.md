## Where the remaining differences come from

1. **The angle math is identical.** The JS kinematics reproduce the reference functions to ~1e-13 on the
   same points.
2. **The model is identical.** Fed byte-identical OpenCV frames, the browser and Python *Tasks API*
   agree to 0.01° on the first two frames. After that, frame-to-frame tracking and smoothing differ between
   the JS 1.1.0 and Python 0.10.13 runtimes, and individual frames drift apart (knee median |Δ| ≈ 4°).
   Python legacy vs. Python Tasks on the same frames differ by the same amount (excluded frames: 64 vs 7).
3. **HDR colour.** Chrome converts HLG/Dolby Vision to sRGB for display; OpenCV/FFmpeg passes the 10-bit
   values through without that conversion, so the model sees different images. On the HDR clip the browser
   reads near-knee peak swing flexion ~4° higher than both Python runs. On SDR the summaries agree.

**Consequence:** the tibia-gate *count* is not reproducible across runtimes (it depends on which frames
the tracker glitches on), and whole-clip min/max are sensitive to a handful of frames. Prefer per-stride
medians/percentiles for clinical numbers. iPhone clips are HDR by default.

# Gait events and event-based metrics (Milestone 3, side view)

Clip `IMG_0639_2.mov`, filmed from the runner's left (near leg = left). Landmarks from the GPU path;
events and metrics re-computed from the cached landmarks (`test-data/debug/IMG_0639_2.rows.json`).
Every 2nd frame of a 119.95 fps clip is analysed, so each event is resolved to ±16.7 ms and contact
times move in 16.7 ms steps. Frame numbers below are source frames; contact = TO − IC.

## Events (`js/pipeline/events.js`)

Contact = lowest foot point within a tolerance (0.3 foot lengths) of that stride's belt level.
IC / TO = first / last contact frame; MS = ankle under the hip midpoint. Contact sheet reviewed by the
clinician: near-leg IC, MS and TO correct; far-leg IC and TO look right but far-leg MS is wrong
(far-leg landmark problem, known limitation).

**Belt slant.** Not camera roll. Image horizontals (44 Hough lines, mostly treadmill rails and belt
edges) have a median of 0.0° (IQR 0.0–1.2°). The foot-contact belt fits were not significant: near leg
−0.03° (SE ±1.6°, n 81), far leg −4.3° (SE ±2.6°, n 80; far-foot landmark error). The slope is now used
only when it is clear (≥ 20 strides and |slope| > 2 SE); otherwise the belt is taken as level. No roll
correction is applied to "vs vertical" angles: the estimated roll is ~0°, and a belt-fit-based
correction would add ±3° of noise.

## IC-sensitivity rule (`measurements.js`, `analysis.js`)

IC-dependent metrics (knee flexion, tibial inclination, foot inclination, foot-to-COM and trunk lean,
all at IC, plus trunk change from IC to peak hip extension) are recomputed at tolerances 0.2, 0.3 and
0.4. If the green/yellow/red status differs anywhere across the sweep, the range is shown as
"borderline, IC-sensitive": no flag, no score weight, no pattern trigger, no L/R difference.

## Known limitation: short clips

On `side_excerpt_8s.mov` (8 s of the same footage, separate pose run) only 4 of 9 near-leg strides
pass: on 5 strides a 3-frame landmark wobble breaks the stance run early (contact 7–14% of the
cycle). With quality below the floor, every event metric correctly reads "not assessed". Variants that
recover the excerpt (percentile belt level, 3-frame gap bridging) either moved IC on 37–47 of the
full clip's approved strides or lost valid strides there, so none was adopted. Until event detection is
made more robust, record at least ~30 s of steady running.

## Hip-extension window ends at toe-off + 50 ms (near leg)

The late-stance window used to end at the inter-ankle signal's minimum + 10% of the cycle. That minimum
depends on far-foot landmarks; under it, peaks fell a median of 2 source frames after TO but 22 of 66
fell more than 50 ms after TO (up to 167 ms, i.e. early swing). The window now ends at detected TO +
50 ms; strides without a valid near-leg TO are not measured.

| | Strides | Median | IQR |
|---|---|---|---|
| Before (signal-based end) | 81/83 | 18.2° | 11.5–24.7° |
| After (TO + 50 ms) | 55/83 | 14.3° | 9.6–20.4° |

15 strides changed, all lower (median −6.8°, range −0.1° to −21.7°); 39 unchanged; 27 no longer
measured (15 without a valid TO, 12 still rising at TO + 50 ms, so no interior peak); 1 gained. Example:
cycle 70 went from 17.4° at frame 6172 (18 frames after TO) to 6.2° at its TO (6154).

## Foot-height traces (cycles 10, 25, 40, 54)

`test-data/debug/foot_height_traces_IMG_0639_2.png`. In all 67 valid strides TO is the last on-belt
frame (the foot is off the belt on the next frame), never the search-window edge; IC is never the

## Pose input resolution

Pose runs on frames downscaled to 0.45 (486×864 for a 1080×1920 clip). MediaPipe crops a region
around the person and resizes it to its 256×256 landmark model, and the runner is well over 256 px tall
at 0.45 scale, so extra input resolution adds little. On the 8 s side excerpt, full resolution was not
more precise: tibia-length MAD 6.9% vs 7.1%, heel-to-toe length MAD 17% vs 13%, hip jitter equal.

## Far leg on a lateral clip (2026-10-09)
15 frames at the far (right) leg's own detected IC / midstance / toe-off (6 strides; 3 detected far
midstances fell on the IC frame), both legs hand-marked blind, near/far decided by occlusion.

- **Left/right label swaps:** in 17 of 65 far-leg point checks (26%) the app's far-leg point was

- **Far events:** at 1 of 6 detected far ICs the far foot was not on the belt (event wrong, points

- **Unswapped frames:** far knee flexion bias ≈ −8° (toe-off frames −19 to +3°); far hip hidden

A fixed offset cannot correct swaps of this size. **Decision: far-leg values stay out of the
output.** Bilateral side-view values need a clip from each side.

  Knee-based side metrics are therefore validated on C0 only.
- Trunk lean error has opposite signs for runners facing left (C0, −6°) and right (T, +7°), i.e. the
  same tilt in image space: an image-space bias between the app's shoulder/hip points and the hand
  marks. Not correctable without a better reference; trunk lean stays medium confidence.
- Ankle dorsiflexion: 21° off on C0 (heel landmark on the red shoe), 4° on T1–T3.
- Metrics with error above ~5° are not fixable with the single 2D camera and these landmarks;
  their confidence is capped automatically (reconcile.js `VALIDATION_ERROR`: > 5 → medium,
  > 10 → low).

### Whole-leg anchor: leave-one-client-out
Offsets fitted on the other clients' hand marks and applied to the held-out client:
- offsets fitted on T1–T3 point the opposite way to C0's (hip +0.12 thigh lengths forward and
  0.21 down vs C0 −0.16 back);
- on C0, the T-fitted offsets doubled hip-extension error (8 → 19°); on T1–T3 the C0 offsets raised
  ankle-dorsiflexion error from 3–4° to 10–12° and trunk-lean error from 7 to 10°.
**Decision: anchor off for all clips.** A fixed landmark correction does not transfer between camera
setups/runners; it would need per-setup calibration (clinician clicks on each new setup).
