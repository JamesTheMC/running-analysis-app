Blind hand marking (dev only)

Mark joint centres on frames picked from the clip's gait events; the app's own landmarks are hidden. Side view: shoulder, hip, knee, ankle, heel, toe of the leg facing the camera. Rear/front: both sides. Keys: z = undo, ← → = frame. Marks save automatically to test-data/debug (local, gitignored); python3 tools/revalidate.py compares them with the app.
