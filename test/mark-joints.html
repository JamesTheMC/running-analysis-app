Mark the runner's joints

Side view. The runner faces right; mark the leg nearest the camera (the runner's right leg: it is drawn in front where the legs cross). Click the four points in order (frames you already marked only need the heel); the next point is selected automatically. Your marks save as you go (this computer only).

1. Hip
Centre of the hip joint: about level with the bony bump on the side of the hip (greater trochanter), slightly in front of and above it.
2. Knee
Centre of the knee joint: halfway between the front and back of the knee, at the joint line (not the kneecap).
3. Ankle
Outer ankle bone (lateral malleolus), or your best estimate above the shoe collar.
4. Heel
Back of the heel: the rearmost point of the shoe's heel, at the bottom (where it meets the sole).

Tip: zoom the page (pinch or ⌘+) for precision. Click a step to re-place that point.
